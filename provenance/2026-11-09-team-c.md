## 7. Historical DPS comparison

All three configurations were evaluated using six trials under comparable benchmark conditions.

| Configuration | Median DPS | Change vs. previous |
|---|---:|---:|
| Original Team C — Xingqiu | 30,450 | Baseline |
| Initial Team C — Furina Lv. 44 | 31,708 | +4.13% |
| Improved Team C — Furina Lv. 80 | 46,835.5 | +47.71% |

**Total improvement versus the original Xingqiu team: +53.81%.**

The major performance increase occurred after investing in Furina and improving the existing team, rather than immediately upon replacing Xingqiu.

The precise contribution of each individual upgrade cannot be isolated from these benchmarks because several development variables changed between experiments.

## 8. Interpretation

### Finding 1 — Substantial sustained DPS improvement

Median DPS increased from 31,708 to 46,835.5, a gain of approximately 47.7%.

This represents a major improvement in measured team output.

### Finding 2 — More distributed damage

Skirk's share of overall damage decreased from approximately 90% to 70%.

This does not indicate that Skirk became weaker. Instead, Furina and Escoffier contributed much more substantial off-field damage.

### Finding 3 — Peak single-hit damage changed modestly

The strongest hit increased from 41,079 in the initial Furina trials to 43,548 in the current trials.

The increase in total DPS is much larger than the increase in peak single-hit damage.

This suggests that improved damage across the rotation is responsible for much of the observed gain.

### Finding 4 — Significant improvement potential remains

Neither Skirk nor Furina currently has a complete intended 4-piece artifact set.

Furina's Skill and Burst are only Lv. 4.

These are clear avenues for additional investment, although their exact impact on DPS requires further testing.

### Finding 5 — Survivability remains a separate concern

Earlier field testing identified defensive weaknesses compared with the Xingqiu team:

- Furina's HP drain increases healing demands.
- Losing Xingqiu reduces interruption resistance and incoming damage mitigation.
- Dahlia's shield timing can leave portions of the rotation exposed.
- Escoffier's healing needs to compensate for both Furina's HP drain and incoming enemy damage.

These benchmark results measure damage output, not long-term survival or uninterrupted rotations in real encounters.

## 9. Recommended progression priorities

1. Complete Furina's 4-piece Golden Troupe set.
2. Upgrade Furina's Elemental Skill and Burst talents.
3. Complete Skirk's 4-piece Deep Gallery set.
4. Review Escoffier's healing performance and Energy Recharge requirements.
5. Improve Dahlia's shield reliability and supporting equipment.
6. Repeat six controlled trials after significant upgrades.
7. Monitor whether the team can consistently exceed 50,000 median DPS.

## 10. Final conclusion

The November 9 benchmark establishes a new Team C performance record:

**46,835.5 median DPS — approximately 53.8% above the original Xingqiu composition.**

This improvement was achieved while Skirk and Furina still lacked complete intended artifact sets.

The next objective is to determine how much additional performance can be gained through artifact completion and talent investment, without sacrificing defensive reliability.
