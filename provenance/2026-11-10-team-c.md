## 9. Interpretation

### Finding 1 — Major performance milestone

Team C achieved a new median DPS record of 66,325.

The worst November 10 run, at 62,182 DPS, exceeded the best November 9 run of 47,524 DPS.

This demonstrates a clear separation between the two benchmark distributions.
