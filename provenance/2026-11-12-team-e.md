**Date:** 2026-11-12

Establish a reproducible baseline for Team E following initial exploration of Mitya's Charged Attack rotation.

## 6. Preliminary comparison

Earlier single test:

- DPS: 8,684
- Total damage: 173,893
- Duration: 20.02 seconds
- Strongest hit: 19,129
- Mitya damage: 100,129
- Escoffier damage: 67,403
- Traveler damage: 843
- Valeriy damage: 5,518

New six-run median:

- DPS: 11,339
- Duration: 23.04 seconds

**Nominal DPS increase: 30.57%.**

This is not a controlled before/after estimate because:

1. The preliminary result was only one trial.
2. Duration increased by approximately 15%.
3. The rotation was being refined.
4. Some trials displayed differing elemental damage and CRIT bonuses.

Therefore, do not attribute the entire improvement exclusively to the extra Charged Attack.

## 7. Findings

### Finding 1 — Functional Stellar-Conduct foundation

Team E successfully produced repeated Stellar-Conduct reactions and maintained a functional damage rotation.

### Finding 2 — Longer rotation appears promising

The tested longer damage window achieved substantially greater total damage than the preliminary test, supporting further experimentation with an additional Charged Attack.

Its actual efficiency benefit remains to be established with controlled comparisons.

### Finding 3 — Mitya is the primary damage contributor

Mitya dealt 63.82% of total damage, despite a Lv50 weapon and provisional artifacts.

### Finding 4 — Escoffier is carrying considerable early-stage damage

Escoffier contributed 33.94% of measured total damage.

Her developed build allows Team E to function while Mitya and Valeriy remain significantly underinvested.

### Finding 5 — Significant development headroom remains

Mitya needs a stronger weapon, higher talents and a proper artifact set.

Valeriy requires character levels and improved equipment.

Traveler also needs support-oriented equipment optimization.

## 8. Next steps

1. Upgrade Mitya's Dawning Frost from Lv50.
2. Prioritize Mitya's Normal Attack talent.
3. Develop Mitya's artifact set.
4. Raise Valeriy toward Lv70 and upgrade his equipment.
5. Replace Traveler's Prototype Rancour with a more appropriate support weapon when available.
6. Compare the longer Charged Attack sequence against a shorter rotation under equal test durations.
7. Repeat a six-run benchmark after the next meaningful build improvements.

## 9. Conclusion

**Team E initial benchmark: 11,339 median DPS across six trials.**

Peak performance: **12,049 DPS and 277,599 total damage**.

The team is functional but still in an early investment phase. The principal damage sources are Mitya and Escoffier, with considerable room for development.

The next benchmark should evaluate both equipment progression and whether the extra Charged Attack consistently improves rotation efficiency.
